# Market Analysis

Tracks publicly traded companies, collects their price history from market data providers, and analyses it to produce trading recommendations.

## Language

### Market data

**Company**:
A publicly traded business whose price history is tracked and analysed.
_Avoid_: Stock, Symbol, Security

**Ticker**:
The unique market identifier of a Company, such as `AAPL`.
_Avoid_: Symbol, Stock code

**Stock Quote**:
The open, high, low and close prices, volume and trade count of one Company over one short interval of trading.
_Avoid_: Quote (alone), Bar, Candle, Price
